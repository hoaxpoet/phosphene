## What this rules out

Ordinary visible Witchlight rendering does not monotonically leak a drawable per frame. The
two `currentDrawable` lookups recorded per presented frame resolve to the same unique drawable,
which is presented and recycled. Local track teardown/startup and preset-state reset also
completed without disturbing the balance. A deterministic time-to-exhaustion at approximately
3.6 minutes is refuted.

## What remains open

BUG-085 remains an intermittent P1. Because no freeze occurred, HANG.2 did not produce the
pending request and last imbalance needed to identify an owner. No render behavior changes are
justified from this control. On the next live freeze, leave the process running and execute
`Scripts/capture_hang.sh` before force-quit; that artifact, not another speculative change, is
the input to diagnosis.
