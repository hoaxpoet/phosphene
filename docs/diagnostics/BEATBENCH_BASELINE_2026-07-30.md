Metric definitions and windows: the `beatbench` skill. F and downbeat F use
±70 ms one-to-one matching; Cemgil σ = 40 ms; continuity tolerance 17.5 %.
AMLt accepts double/half/offbeat readings, CMLt does not — a large AMLt−CMLt
gap means the grid found the pulse but not the metrical level.
