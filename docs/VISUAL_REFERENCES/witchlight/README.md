# Visual References — Witchlight

**Family:** `abstract` (provisional — settled in WL.2 against `docs/DECISIONS.md` D-123)
**Render pipeline:** `feedback + particles` — a CPU-side stroke ring buffer (`WitchlightStroke`, `ParticleGeometry`) drawn as beaded sprites plus a thin line, over a composite `witchlight_sky_fragment` ground (parallax stars, violet bloom, star suppression, head flare). Closest structural template is **Filigree**. See `docs/presets/WITCHLIGHT_DESIGN.md` §7; **no new render pass, fragment-buffer slot or GPU-contract change.**
**Rubric:** lightweight — a single luminous stroke on a near-black ground; the §12 3D-surface / material-count matrix does not apply. The load-bearing gates are Matt's live M7 and the flash budget (`WITCHLIGHT_DESIGN.md` §5).
**Last curated:** 2026-07-31 (WL.1). Eleven license-verified positives/anti-references from Wikimedia Commons + two frames of Matt's own render of the inspiration source (see *Provenance*).

> **This preset is a Milkdrop-**inspired** uplift under Phase MD (D-113 / `MILKDROP_STRATEGY.md` §12), not a port.** Its inspiration source is `martin - witchcraft reloaded`. `00` is a still from Matt's local render of that source. Read `00` as a **positive reference on visual register** (beaded luminous stroke, deep-space ground, violet bloom, flare at the head) and as an **anti-reference on path and colour** — the source's stroke is a random walk re-seeded on beats, and its per-segment hue is an arbitrary random unit vector. Witchlight's divergence axis under D-121 is the **dominant motion model** (and consequentially palette character): the pen tip is driven by the track's harmonic and spectral state. See `WITCHLIGHT_DESIGN.md` §1 and D-209.

## How to read this folder

None of these is a pixel-target (D-064). Each is read **only for the trait its annotation names**. The register we are aiming at is *long-exposure light-painting photographed against a deep-space sky* — a Witchlight frame should belong in the same visual conversation as `01` (a legible figure written in the air with a burning point) lit against the ground of `07`, never in the same conversation as `10` (an unreadable tangle).

**The single most important thing this folder encodes:** compare `01` against `10`. Both are the same physical apparatus — a sparkler moved through air during one long exposure. `01` reads as *intent* because the hand moved slowly and smoothly relative to the exposure; `10` reads as *noise* because it did not. Nothing about the rendering differs. **Legibility of a light-painted stroke is a property of the path's smoothness and speed, not of its shading.** That is the trait that makes or breaks this preset, and it is why §3 of the design doc has to name a relaxation/rate-governance mechanism explicitly rather than leaving smoothing as a tuning constant.

## Mandatory traits

Witchlight is not certified until every one of these holds. Cited by filename per `SHADER_CRAFT.md §2.3`.

- [ ] **1. The stroke reads as a deliberate gesture, not a scribble** (`01`, not `10`). Operationalised in `WITCHLIGHT_DESIGN.md` §3 as a named relaxation + rate-governance mechanism on the pen path, not as a tuning constant.
- [ ] **2. Monotonic brightness falloff along the trail, with an unambiguous head** (`02`). The oldest visible bead must be dimmer than every newer one; the head must be the brightest point on the stroke at all times.
- [ ] **3. Two-part radial profile on the line — hot core, cooler and wider halo** (`08`, `09`). A single-colour line at two intensities fails this trait.
- [ ] **4. Stroke thickness and bead density vary along the stroke** (`05`, `02`). Constant-width output is trait `11` (anti).
- [ ] **5. Deep-space ground: near-black field, dense small stars in ≥3 parallax depth layers, one soft low-frequency violet bloom** (`07` for the field, `06` for the bloom). Pale-tone share ≤ 30 % (`SHADER_CRAFT.md §12.7`).
- [ ] **6. Bounded head flare** (`03`, and NOT `12`). Numeric ceilings — luminance, spatial extent, minimum re-fire interval, flashes/second — in `WITCHLIGHT_DESIGN.md` §5.
- [ ] **7. Colour carries information** (anti-`00`). A bead's hue is set by the harmonic state at the moment it was laid down, so the ribbon's colour banding is the track's colour history. Arbitrary per-segment hue fails this trait even if it looks identical in a single still.
- [ ] **8. Silence renders non-black** (D-037). The star field, the violet bloom and the residual trail persist at `totalStemEnergy == 0`.

## Anti-references

- `10_anti_tangled_scribble_ball.jpg` — **the tangle.** Path too fast / too erratic for the trail length; output is an unreadable ball. Highest-probability accidental failure. Note the measured risk: `tonal_phase_fifths`' angular rate varies ~10× across tracks (`WITCHLIGHT_DESIGN.md` §2), so an ungoverned mapping produces `01` on one track and `10` on the next.
- `11_anti_uniform_glow_tube.jpg` — **the neon tube.** Constant width, constant brightness, no core/halo, no head.
- `12_anti_source_whiteout_flare.png` — **the whiteout.** Head flare saturating a large fraction of the frame; erases the subject and fails photosensitivity.
- **The source's random path** (anti-read of `00`) — a stroke whose shape is the same species of scribble on every track. Diagnostic: play the same track twice; if the figures differ, the path is not being driven by the music. Play two different tracks; if the figures are the same species, likewise.
- **The rainbow ribbon** (anti-read of `00`) — hue cycling that is not anchored to a musical quantity. Looks acceptable in a still; carries nothing.
- **The frozen ribbon.** A stroke that stops accumulating (input stalls, or the trail's age window exceeds the emission rate) reads as a static decoration. `RouteCoverageTests` catches a dead route; a *stalled* one needs the multi-frame harness (`PRESET_SESSION_CHECKLIST.md` Part 2).

## Audio routing notes

The measured driver table is `WITCHLIGHT_DESIGN.md` §2 (signed off by Matt 2026-07-31); the routing design and the layer/primitive table are §3.4, and the proposed `audio_routes` manifest is §7.4. What the measurements ruled in and out:

- **Ruled out as a hero driver:** `pulse_amp01` — a silence gate working exactly as designed, sitting at 1.000 on 98.7 % of live frames, so it has no dynamic range to drive a layer with (`pulse_phase01` is the actual steady-pulse driver and measures alive); `vocalsPitchHz` / `vocalsPitchConfidence` — nonzero on 4.5 % of live frames, garnish only.
- **The `spectral_centroid` trap (BUG-027 / CR.1.1, now measured a third time):** real-music range is ≈ 0.04–0.21, not 0–1. Any mapping must be written against the measured p5/p95 band, never `centroid × N`.
- **Deviation semantics only** (D-026). No absolute threshold on an AGC-normalized value anywhere (FA #31).
- **One primitive per visual layer** (FA #67). The pen path, the bead colour, the head flare and the background parallax are four layers and must not share a primitive.
